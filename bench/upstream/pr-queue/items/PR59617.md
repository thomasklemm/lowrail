# Keep the UTC offset in Time#change for times with a zone object

On a `Time` whose zone is an object (`TZInfo::Timezone` or `ActiveSupport::TimeZone`), `change` couldn't always stay in the hour that repeats when clocks go back. TZInfo zones raised `TZInfo::AmbiguousTime`, also from Active Model's datetime cast when it truncates precision. `ActiveSupport::TimeZone` could jump to the other occurrence of that hour.

`change` now tries the time's own UTC offset first and keeps it when the zone agrees. Otherwise the zone decides, as before.

At 00:30 UTC on 2026-10-25, both zones are in the first of two identical local hours:

| Zone | Start | `change(sec: 1)` on main | With this PR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Berlin, `TZInfo::Timezone` | 02:30:00 +0200 | raises `AmbiguousTime` | 02:30:01 +0200 |
| Dublin, `ActiveSupport::TimeZone` | 01:30:00 +0100 | 01:30:01 +0000, an hour later | 01:30:01 +0100 |

Still unchanged: a time in the skipped spring hour raises `PeriodNotFound`, and with a TZInfo zone an ambiguous time raises when neither occurrence has the original offset.
