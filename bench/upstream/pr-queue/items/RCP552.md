# Clarify Performance/Casecmp Unicode and ASCII safety

Clarify why `casecmp(...).zero?` and `casecmp?` are not general replacements for `downcase`/`upcase` comparisons. `casecmp` only folds ASCII letters, while `casecmp?` uses Unicode case folding rather than the original case conversion.

The safety notes now show the Unicode differences, explain why an ASCII literal alone is insufficient (`'ß'.upcase == 'SS'`), and call out literal normalization and matching conversions for ASCII strings. The cop is already disabled by default and marked `Safe: false`; neither its configuration nor its behavior changes. The generated documentation and changelog are included.

Related to #100. This documents the semantic distinction, not the requested switch to `casecmp?`.
