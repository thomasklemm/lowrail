# Retry interrupted jobs that are safe to run again

Stacked on #346, which should merge first; only the [retry change](https://github.com/thomasklemm/once-campfire/compare/feat/solid-queue-cutover...feat/retry-interrupted-jobs) is new here.

With #346, a job that's still running when its worker is stopped or killed is marked failed (`ProcessExitError`, `ProcessMissingError` at the next boot, or `ProcessPrunedError` once the dead worker is pruned) and has to be retried by hand. Solid Queue [deliberately doesn't retry these](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.0/README.md#jobs-interrupted-by-non-graceful-process-death), since the job may be what killed the worker, and leaves a recovery policy for idempotent jobs to the app.

This adds a recurring task that retries such a job every minute, up to three times, but only for `Room::DestroyJob` and `ActiveStorage::PurgeJob`. Room deletion removes messages one at a time and the room last; rerunning a purge discards an already-purged blob. Other jobs are left alone: they could deliver or broadcast twice, or remove a user's content after the user was unbanned.

`FailedExecution#retry` resets the job's execution count, so the task does the retry itself and counts it in `executions`. Jobs that failed with an error of their own are left alone. It's a sweep rather than a `fail_many_claimed.solid_queue` subscriber, so it also catches failures whose event was missed (the supervisor itself killed, say) and spaces retries a minute apart instead of rerunning a job that may have killed its worker straight away.
