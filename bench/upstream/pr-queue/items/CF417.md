# Retry interrupted jobs that are safe to run again

Stacked on #346, which should merge first; only the last commit (`9b11cb7`) is new here.

With #346, a job still running when its worker is stopped or killed is marked failed (`ProcessExitError`, `ProcessMissingError` at the next boot, or `ProcessPrunedError` once the dead worker is pruned), and someone has to retry it by hand. Solid Queue [deliberately leaves this to the app](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/README.md#jobs-interrupted-by-non-graceful-process-death), since the job may be what killed the worker.

This adds a recurring task that retries such a job once a minute, at most three times, for the jobs that are safe to run again from the start: `Room::DestroyJob` (messages go one at a time and the room last) and `ActiveStorage::PurgeJob`. Webhooks, Web Push and broadcasts would be delivered twice, and banned-content removal could reach a user unbanned since, so they stay failed, as do jobs that failed with an error of their own.
