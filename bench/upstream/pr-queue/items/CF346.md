# Replace Resque with Solid Queue

Jobs move from Resque in the container's Redis to Solid Queue in its own SQLite database. That Redis doesn't persist (`save ""`), so today a restart or an update drops every queued job, such as the file purges of a room deletion. With Solid Queue they're still there when the container starts again. Jobs still in Resque at the upgrade are lost, as on any restart today.

The queue database is backed up and restored with the primary, so a room deletion or a banned user's content removal still queued at the backup runs after a restore. The two are copied one after the other and the app writes them in separate commits, so a job enqueued while the backup runs can be missing from it: a room deleted in that moment comes back closed and without members, with its messages. A job running at the backup comes back claimed by its process and fails once that process is pruned as dead.

The worker runs three threads in one process, as Solid Queue's installer sets it up, with its own pool of five connections.
