    Don't compare owner conditions when joining an association without a scope
    
    To detect conditions that the association scope removed, join_scope
    subtracts where clauses before and after merging the scope items, also
    when there are none. Comparing predicates can call into their bind
    values: Array#- hashes both sides once they are large enough, and
    QueryAttribute#hash and #== serialize the value. So a join whose target
    default scope has an out of range condition raised
    ActiveModel::RangeError instead of matching no records.
    
    Skip the comparison when the association has no scope items, since
    nothing can have removed a condition.
