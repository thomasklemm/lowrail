Test that owner conditions are serialized when the query is built

Comparing where clauses hashes their predicates once both sides have
more than 16, which serializes the bind values. Checking the owner
conditions before the association scope runs, or for joins that have no
scope, serialized them earlier than 8.1 did: a join raised
ActiveModel::RangeError for an out of range default scope condition, and
an association scope changing how its keys serialize no longer applied.

Check the owner conditions only when an association scope was merged

Since the association scope can no longer remove the conditions tying the
records to the owner, every association scope and join scope builds the
differences between its where clauses before and after merging the scope,
also when the association has no scope and nothing can be removed.

Keep the where clauses and compute the differences only after a scope item
was merged.
